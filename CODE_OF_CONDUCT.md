# Code of conduct

Kindtech projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable.

The last of these matters twice over here. Our tools are used with information about people in difficult circumstances. Never share details of a real service user, client or member of the public in an issue, a pull request, a discussion or an example.

## Reporting a problem

Report a conduct problem privately to the maintainers. On the repository where it happened, go to **Security**, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and mark the report as a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers will review every report, and may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block an account, depending on what happened.
